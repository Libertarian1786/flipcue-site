What FlipCue is

FlipCue estimates what a second-hand item might resell for, and what a marketplace's fees would take out of that. It is a research tool for your own buying decisions.

Estimates are estimates

FlipCue does not appraise anything. Every figure in the app is an estimate built from prices that comparable items are currently listed at, together with fee rates we publish in the app. Actual sale prices vary with condition, timing, photography, season, shipping and luck.

You are responsible for what you buy and what you pay. We are not liable for losses from decisions you make using FlipCue, including items that do not sell or sell for less than estimated.

Identification is automated and can be wrong, which is why the app tells you how confident it is. Treat a low-confidence result as a guess.

Fee rates are checked against each marketplace's published schedule and dated in Settings, but marketplaces change fees without notice and rates vary by category and seller status. Check your own account for what you will actually be charged.
